Konteks

Human Activity Recognition (Neural Network)

Data TensorFlow/KerasNumPyWearable Sensors

Konteks

Pendekatan

Memproses 92.000+ entri dataset MHEALTH (gyroscope ankle & wrist), normalisasi & segmentasi, neural network (64 neuron, ReLU + Softmax).

98.77% Akurasi (macro F1 0.9877) — klasifikasi sempurna untuk kelas Sitting.
